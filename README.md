### 🧑‍💻 Who Am I?

I'm a **Senior AI Systems Engineer** with 5+ years building autonomous agents, LLM-powered systems, and the production infrastructure that keeps them reliable, safe, and fast. My work sits at the intersection of **agentic AI, retrieval systems, and MLOps**:

- **Agentic AI & Orchestration** — designing multi-agent systems (ReAct + Reflexion) with LangChain/CrewAI that reason across multiple steps, use tools, and hand off to humans when it matters.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — hybrid BM25 + dense vector retrieval, cross-encoder re-ranking, and Pinecone/FAISS vector search tuned for sub-200ms latency at 50k+ document scale.
- **AI Safety & Guardrails** — layered guardrail systems (NeMo Guardrails, prompt-injection defense, PII/PHI anonymization) that cut unsafe or out-of-scope outputs by 70%+.
- **Production MLOps** — shipping and monitoring models on AWS SageMaker, Azure Databricks, Docker, and Kubernetes with CI/CD, drift detection, and canary deployments.
- **Quantitative & Risk ML** — regulated, audit-ready model pipelines (WoE, IV, VIF) for financial services, built on Spark, Snowflake, and Hadoop at scale.

🚀 I like building systems where research-grade ideas (multi-agent reasoning, hallucination detection, explainability) get turned into production tools that are fast, governed, and trustworthy.
